# Live Demo Prompts — BOL Remotion Show & Tell

Use these prompts during the live demo to show how Claude creates motion graphics from plain English. Each one targets a different capability. Ordered for maximum impact.

## 1. Motion Typography (warm-up)

"Create a Remotion scene called ClientQuote that animates the text 'AI doesn't replace strategy. It accelerates it.' Use the BOL orange (#F85E32) on dark charcoal. Words should appear one at a time with a subtle spring animation, then the whole quote scales down as 'BOL Agency' fades in below with a cyan (#7BEDF8) underline."

## 2. Animated Stat Counter

"Create a scene called StatReveal that counts up from 0 to 847% with an easing curve over 2 seconds, with the label 'increase in qualified leads' appearing below after the counter finishes. Use a large bold number in white on black, with a subtle particle trail behind the number as it climbs."

## 3. Bar Chart Race

"Create a scene called ChannelPerformance that animates a horizontal bar chart comparing four marketing channels: Paid Social, Organic, Email, and Events. The bars should grow from left to right sequentially, with values 42%, 28%, 18%, and 12%. Each bar in a different color from the BOL palette. Add value labels that slide in as each bar reaches its final width."

## 4. Animated Infographic

"Create a scene called FunnelViz that shows a marketing funnel with four stages: Awareness (10,000), Consideration (3,200), Intent (890), and Conversion (340). Animate it top-down, each level sliding in and shrinking to the next width proportionally. Use a gradient from cyan at the top to orange at the bottom. Add the numbers counting up as each level appears."

## 5. Illustrated Icon Animation

"Create a scene called AIWorkflow that shows three icons in a row: a document icon, a brain/AI icon, and a rocket icon. They should be simple SVG illustrations. Animate them appearing left to right with connecting arrows that draw themselves between them. Below each icon, labels fade in: 'Input', 'Process', 'Launch'. The whole thing should feel like a workflow diagram coming to life."

## 6. Data Visualization (donut chart)

"Create a scene called BudgetBreakdown that animates a donut chart showing budget allocation: Creative 35%, Media 30%, Strategy 20%, Analytics 15%. Each segment should sweep in clockwise one at a time with its label and percentage appearing next to it. Use BOL brand colors. Add a total budget of '$2.4M' in the center of the donut that scales in after all segments are drawn."

## 7. Kinetic Text + Visual Composition

"Create a scene called BeforeAfter that splits the frame vertically. The left side is labeled 'Before AI' in a muted gray with a slow, boring clock ticking animation. The right side is labeled 'With BOL + AI' in bright orange with energetic particles and a speedometer needle jumping to the right. The dividing line should sweep from left to reveal the right side dramatically."

## 8. Animated Timeline (the closer)

"Create a scene called CampaignTimeline that shows a horizontal timeline with 5 milestones: Week 1 'Discovery', Week 2 'Strategy', Week 4 'Launch', Week 8 'Optimize', Week 12 'Scale'. A glowing dot should travel along the timeline, pausing at each milestone as the label pops up. When it reaches 'Scale', the dot explodes into a starburst of BOL orange particles."

---

## Demo Tips

- Start with #1 (safe, impressive, fast)
- Hit #3 or #4 in the middle (data viz is where jaws drop)
- Close with #8 (timeline with particle explosion is the mic drop)
- Skip any that feel slow in the moment. Better to nail 5 great ones than rush 8.
