# Live Demo Prompts — BOL Remotion Show & Tell

Use these prompts during the live demo to show how Claude creates motion graphics from plain English. Each one targets a different capability. Ordered for maximum impact.

## 1. Motion Typography (warm-up)

"Create a Remotion scene called ClientQuote that animates the text 'AI doesn't replace strategy. It accelerates it.' Use the BOL orange (#F85E32) on dark charcoal. Words should appear one at a time with a subtle spring animation, then the whole quote scales down as 'BOL Agency' fades in below with a cyan (#7BEDF8) underline."

## 2. Animated Stat Counter

"Create a scene called StatReveal that counts up from 0 to 847% with an easing curve over 2 seconds, with the label 'increase in qualified leads' appearing below after the counter finishes. Use a large bold number in white on black, with a subtle particle trail behind the number as it climbs."

## 3. Bar Chart Race

"Create a scene called ChannelPerformance that animates a horizontal bar chart comparing four marketing channels: Paid Social, Organic, Email, and Events. The bars should grow from left to right sequentially, with values 42%, 28%, 18%, and 12%. Each bar in a different color from the BOL palette. Add value labels that slide in as each bar reaches its final width."

## 4. Animated Infographic

"Create a scene called FunnelViz that shows a marketing funnel with four stages: Awareness (10,000), Consideration (3,200), Intent (890), and Conversion (340). Animate it top-down, each level sliding in and shrinking to the next width proportionally. Use a gradient from cyan at the top to orange at the bottom. Add the numbers counting up as each level appears."

## 5. Illustrated Icon Animation

"Create a scene called AIWorkflow that shows three icons in a row: a document icon, a brain/AI icon, and a rocket icon. They should be simple SVG illustrations. Animate them appearing left to right with connecting arrows that draw themselves between them. Below each icon, labels fade in: 'Input', 'Process', 'Launch'. The whole thing should feel like a workflow diagram coming to life."

## 6. Data Visualization (donut chart)

"Create a scene called BudgetBreakdown that animates a donut chart showing budget allocation: Creative 35%, Media 30%, Strategy 20%, Analytics 15%. Each segment should sweep in clockwise one at a time with its label and percentage appearing next to it. Use BOL brand colors. Add a total budget of '$2.4M' in the center of the donut that scales in after all segments are drawn."

## 7. Kinetic Text + Visual Composition

"Create a scene called BeforeAfter that splits the frame vertically. The left side is labeled 'Before AI' in a muted gray with a slow, boring clock ticking animation. The right side is labeled 'With BOL + AI' in bright orange with energetic particles and a speedometer needle jumping to the right. The dividing line should sweep from left to reveal the right side dramatically."

## 8. Animated Timeline (the closer)

"Create a scene called CampaignTimeline that shows a horizontal timeline with 5 milestones: Week 1 'Discovery', Week 2 'Strategy', Week 4 'Launch', Week 8 'Optimize', Week 12 'Scale'. A glowing dot should travel along the timeline, pausing at each milestone as the label pops up. When it reaches 'Scale', the dot explodes into a starburst of BOL orange particles."

## 9. Lower Thirds (broadcast-style speaker plate)

"Create a scene called SpeakerLowerThird at 1920x1080. A broadcast-style lower-third graphic enters from the left along a thin BOL orange line that draws across the bottom-left third of the screen. Once the line lands, the name 'Sarah Chen' slides up in large bold white text, then the title 'Director of Strategy · BOL Agency' fades in below in cyan with thin-weight type. Hold for 4 seconds, then everything reverses out the way it came in. Add a small cyan dot pulsing next to the name like a 'live' indicator. Pure graphite background. The whole thing should feel like the bottom of a CNN interview, but tasteful."

## 10. Hero Shot (cinematic brand opener)

"Create a scene called HeroOpener at 1920x1080. Start with a graphite background. After a beat of stillness, the BOL letterforms 'BOL' materialize in the center one at a time — each letter scales up from 0 with a snappy spring while rotating 30 degrees into place. Behind the letters, a BOL-orange WebGL light leak sweeps across the frame at peak. The tagline 'Strategy at the speed of AI' types itself out below the logo, character by character with a blinking cursor, in cyan. After the tagline finishes, hold for 1 second, then everything scales out with a smooth easing as the screen fades to white. Total length: 8 seconds. Cinematic, confident, no clip art."

## 11. Explainer (multi-beat concept walkthrough)

"Create a scene called HowGEOWorks at 1920x1080, 25 seconds long. Three sequential beats wired through `<Series>`:

Beat 1 (8s) — 'OLD WAY' in cyan eyebrow text, then big graphite headline 'You ranked for keywords.' A simulated Google search bar appears with three search results sliding in below it. The search bar dims and the words 'keyword game' fade in over it.

Beat 2 (10s) — 'NEW WAY' eyebrow, headline 'Now you have to be the answer.' An LLM chat box appears with a question being typed: 'best AI agency for B2B?' Below, content fragments (a blog post card, a podcast card, a LinkedIn post card) flow into the chat box from three directions. The LLM's reply animates in: 'Based on multiple sources, BOL Agency…'.

Beat 3 (7s) — 'THE SHIFT' eyebrow in BOL orange, headline 'GEO traffic is up 312%.' A line chart draws across showing GEO surpassing traditional search, with a big '+312%' reveal at the end.

Use BOL brand colors throughout. Each beat should feel like a separate slide that flows into the next via a clean fade or wipe transition."

## 12. Data Visualization (KPI dashboard)

"Create a scene called QuarterlyKPIs at 1920x1080. A 4-card dashboard for Q3 2025. Title at the top: 'Q3 2025 Performance' in graphite, scaling in first.

Below the title, a 2×2 grid of cards. Each card has: a metric label (small, gray), a big number (large, bold, graphite or BOL orange), a delta indicator (+/- vs Q2 in cyan or orange), and a sparkline below. Stagger the cards entering with a spring-based offset so they flow in top-left → top-right → bottom-left → bottom-right.

The four cards:

1. 'Conversion Rate' · 12.4% · +2.1pt vs Q2 · sparkline trending up in cyan
2. 'Pipeline Value' · $4.2M · +18% vs Q2 · sparkline trending up in cyan
3. 'Avg Deal Size' · $85K · +12% vs Q2 · sparkline trending up in cyan
4. 'Cycle Time' · 28 days · -6 days vs Q2 · sparkline trending DOWN in BOL orange (down is good for cycle time)

Each sparkline draws itself across the card in sync with the card's entrance using @remotion/paths. After all four cards are in, a small caption fades in at the bottom: 'All metrics improved vs Q2.'"

---

## Demo Tips

- Start with #1 (safe, impressive, fast)
- Hit #3 or #4 in the middle (data viz is where jaws drop)
- Close with #8 (timeline with particle explosion is the mic drop)
- **Use #9 (lower thirds) when the audience is execs** — broadcast aesthetic always plays
- **Use #10 (hero opener) when you have time for cinema** — it's the most "After Effects-y" demo, great proof point
- **Use #11 (explainer) for SaaS / agency new-business meetings** — concept videos are the highest-value format in pitches
- **Use #12 (KPI dashboard) for quarterly reviews / client share-outs** — same template, swap data, ship
- Skip any that feel slow in the moment. Better to nail 5 great ones than rush 12.
